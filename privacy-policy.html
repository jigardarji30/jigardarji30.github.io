Privacy Policy for Neon Survivor

App: Neon Survivor (com.jigardarji.survivor)
Developer: Jigar Darji
Effective date: 6 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Neon Survivor ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you play it. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is a single-player, offline game: it has no user accounts, no login, and we do not ask you for any personal information.

1. Information we collect directly

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you ourselves. Your game progress and high scores are saved only on your own device and are never sent to us or any server we control.

2. Information collected by third parties (advertising)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect and process certain information, including:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • Approximate, non-precise device and usage data (device model, operating system, IP address, and general ad-interaction data)

This information is used to deliver ads (including, where permitted, personalized ads), to measure ad performance, and to prevent fraud. We do not have access to, and do not store, this data ourselves.

For details on how Google uses this information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Permissions used

  • Internet access — required to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — used by Google AdMob as described above.

4. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (Android).
  • Where required by law (e.g. GDPR/EU), a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are served, and you may decline.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share any personal data, because we do not collect any. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained and handled according to Google's own policies linked above.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
