Existing applications

Not sure what's wrong with your existing application?

When an application has become slow, fragile or expensive to change, the first useful step is usually a clear picture of why. A Technical Audit & Modernization Roadmap gives you that in writing, before committing to any rebuild.

What I review

  • Architecture and overall structure
  • Code quality and maintainability
  • Performance and slow paths
  • Database schema and query behaviour
  • Dependencies and framework versions
  • Security risks
  • Infrastructure and deployment
  • Accumulated technical debt

What you get

  • A prioritised list of issues, worst first
  • Risk assessment for each one
  • Recommended improvements
  • A refactoring roadmap you can act on in stages
  • Effort estimates per item
  • A technical walkthrough call with your team

Scoped and quoted as a paid engagement, sized to the application. You keep the roadmap whether or not I do the work that follows.

FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting.

If something here isn't covered, ask me directly. I'd rather over-explain than have you guess.

What type of projects do you work on?
Complex web applications rather than brochure sites: e-commerce and B2B commerce platforms, SaaS products, customer portals, internal business systems, and API-heavy integrations. Most involve either building something custom or improving an application that already exists.
Can you work with an existing codebase?
Yes, and it's a large share of what I do. I work through your normal pull request and code review process, follow the conventions already in the repo, and don't propose rewrites when a targeted fix will do. Legacy Laravel, Symfony and plain PHP codebases are familiar territory.
Do you work with agencies?
Yes. I work as a white-label development partner: you keep the client relationship and present the work as your team's, while I handle delivery inside your repository and follow your existing process. I don't approach your clients during a project or after it.
Can you work under NDA?
Yes, and I'm happy to sign yours rather than ask you to use mine. If you don't have a template I can supply a straightforward one covering scope, payment, confidentiality and IP. All code and associated IP transfer to you on final payment.
What engagement models do you offer?
Three: fixed scope for clearly defined projects, dedicated development for ongoing capacity, and a longer-term development partnership for agencies and companies needing continuous technical support. Engagement and pricing depend on scope, complexity and the capacity required.
Can you work with teams in different time zones?
Yes. I work with clients across the US, UK, Europe and Australia, with a Git-based workflow, written specs and async updates so progress doesn't depend on us being online together. Calls are scheduled in your timezone, and I keep daily overlap with European hours and US mornings.
What happens after the initial project?
Most clients continue with either ad-hoc improvements or reserved monthly capacity. Everything lives in your repository with current documentation and a deployment runbook, so you're never dependent on me being available.

Have a project that's stuck?

Tell me what you're building, what isn't working, or what you need to improve. I read every message myself and reply within 24 hours.
